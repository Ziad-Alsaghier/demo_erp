## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive, high-trust ERP experience designed for high-throughput distribution, logistics management, and supply chain operations. The visual atmosphere merges deep oceanic maritime tones with luminous glassmorphism, evoking precision, operational mastery, and modern algorithmic intelligence.

### Visual Aesthetic & Metaphor
- **Deep Marine Depths:** Foundations are rooted in abyssal navy tones, removing harsh display glare across 12-hour shifts while maintaining infinite visual depth.
- **Atmospheric Luminosity:** Strategic ambient gradients and subtle dynamic orbs (#1E9BE8 at 25%, #5BCBFF at 18%) simulate advanced digital HUDs and navigational cockpit consoles.
- **Structural Glass Architecture:** Tiered frosted glass layers provide physical structural separation between operational modules, live telematics, inventory pipelines, and financial ledgers without adding opaque visual bulk.
- **Enterprise Modernism:** Blends clinical Swiss layout discipline with high-end financial and distribution software gloss, projecting authority, data density, and institutional agility.

## Colors

The palette utilizes deeply layered indigo-navy foundations punctuated by radiant cobalt, cyan, and icy white highlights. The color architecture enforces rigorous visual hierarchy, distinct status visibility, and WCAG AA contrast compliance across dark glass surfaces.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#04122B`):** Deepest structural canvas for background viewport base.
- **Canvas Primary (`#061A3D`):** Primary page background hosting ambient lighting fields.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0B2A5B`):** Structural solid fallback and foundational glass tint for data containers.
- **Surface Accent / Borderline (`#123B7A`):** Low-tier structural dividers, subtle grid frames, and interactive state baselines.

### Brand & Interactive
- **Primary Brand Blue (`#1E9BE8`):** Dominant brand hue, interactive control triggers, primary workflow actions, and data anchors.
- **Hover & Gradient Mid (`#3DB1F5`):** Hover states, interactive shifts, and primary button gradients.
- **Luminous Cyan (`#5BCBFF`):** Active focus rings, selected pipeline paths, ambient glow edges, and primary data highlights.
- **Ice Light Accent (`#E6F4FD`):** High-priority data pills, secondary hover texts, and badge backgrounds.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary text and prominent numeric readouts.

### Functional & Analytics
- **Success (`#2DD4A7`):** Stock in hand, delivered freight, completed batches, positive yield margins.
- **Warning (`#F5A524`):** Threshold breaches, pipeline delays, low inventory reserves.
- **Danger (`#F0546B`):** Critical disruptions, order cancellations, failed shipments, deficit deltas.
- **Info (`#5BCBFF`):** System notifications, route recalculations, automated scheduling advisories.
- **Neutral / Muted (`#8FA3C0`):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, column titles, and structural ticks.

### Data Visualization Array
For multidimensional distribution logistics charts:
`#1E9BE8`, `#5BCBFF`, `#6C7CFF`, `#2DD4A7`, `#F5A524`, `#F0546B`, `#A78BFA`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances executive presentation headers with data-dense enterprise tabular needs.

- **Primary Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a welcoming yet commanding modern posture for top-level operational cockpits, board-level views, and section titles.
- **Body & Controls:** `Inter` handles operational data, form controls, navigation, and nested enterprise records with mechanical neutrality and optimized legibility at micro scales.
- **Arabic Localization:** When running under localized Middle Eastern logistics viewports (e.g., Cairo, Alexandria, MENA hubs), map fonts seamlessly to `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`, preserving relative weight distributions and optical baselines.
- **Tabular & Monospace Rules:** All numeric currency values (EGP), inventory serial numbers, tracking identifiers, quantities, and operational timestamps must explicitly apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to ensure uniform alignment across dense data grids and ledger tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptable 12-column fluid grid system geared for mission-critical operations across 4K logistics displays, dual-monitor desktop setups, tablet warehouse terminals, and mobile dispatch interfaces.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Maximum viewport content bound capped at 1920px for ultrawide dashboards, centering content with fluid margins.
- **Tablet / Terminal (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Sidebars collapse to icon-only rails.
- **Mobile Handheld (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) canvas margin. Cards, metrics, and workflows collapse into single-column vertical stacks.

### Density & Compactness
To accommodate dense enterprise operations, internal table paddings drop to `0.5rem` (`space-sm`) vertical padding when "Compact View" is toggled, while default overview interfaces leverage `1rem` (`space-md`) spacing to preserve breathing room between glass panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses realistic glass refractions and multi-layered backdrop filtration rather than high-contrast solid card fills. Surfaces float over luminous background energy fields.

### Layer 1: Operational Base Cards
- **Usage:** Standard dashboard widgets, KPI cards, primary module containers.
- **Material Recipe:** Background fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, backdrop filter `blur(24px) saturate(140%)`.
- **Borders & Highlights:** Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, top specular reflection highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Shadow:** Extruded ambient drop `0 20px 50px rgba(2, 10, 30, 0.45)`.
- **Corner Curvature:** 20px radius.

### Layer 2: Nested Sub-Tiles & Table Containers
- **Usage:** Data table headers, nested KPI sub-metrics, filter bars, inline control panels.
- **Material Recipe:** Background fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`, backdrop filter `blur(16px)`.
- **Borders & Highlights:** Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, top edge highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Corner Curvature:** 14px radius.

### Layer 3: Overlays, Modals, Flyouts & Command Bar
- **Usage:** Executive command palette (Cmd+K), dispatch drawers, transactional confirmation modals.
- **Material Recipe:** Background fill `rgba(6, 26, 61, 0.85)`, backdrop filter `blur(30px) saturate(160%)`.
- **Borders & Highlights:** Border `1px solid rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.25)`, ambient border glow `0 0 24px rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Shadow:** Deep containment shadow `0 25px 60px rgba(0, 5, 20, 0.75)`.

### Ambient Glows & Swoosh Embellishments
Canvas-level background features two static blurred radial gradients:
1. `radial-gradient(circle at 15% 20%, rgba(30, 155, 232, 0.25) 0%, transparent 45%)`
2. `radial-gradient(circle at 85% 75%, rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.18) 0%, transparent 40%)`
Interactive elements employ localized cyan specular halos upon hover and active focus.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a modern, disciplined corner radius system. Large glass cards use wider radii to soften industrial data density, while nested controls use sharper corners for precise data entry.

- **Primary Glass Cards & Panels:** Standardized at 20px radius (`rounded-xl` extended) to accentuate frosted glass refraction along corners.
- **Nested Control Groups, Tables & Modals:** Standardized at 14px to 16px radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Interactive Inputs, Form Elements, Dropdowns:** Fixed at 8px radius (`rounded`, 0.5rem) ensuring compact density in complex order processing forms.
- **Status Pills, Badges & Interactive Tag Filters:** Fully rounded pill-shaped profiles (`9999px`) for rapid visual scanning.

- **Secondary Glass Button:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, text `#E6F4FD`. Hover: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, border color `#5BCBFF`.
- **Danger Button:** Background `rgba(240, 84, 107, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(240, 84, 107, 0.4)`, text `#F0546B`.

### 2. KPI Cards & Telematics Blocks
- **Container:** Layer 1 glass card profile with 20px radius, containing 24px inner padding.
- **Header:** Label metadata in `label-sm` slate (`#8FA3C0`), uppercase with letter-spacing.
- **Metric Row:** Large tabular readout in `numeric-metric` with currency denotation (e.g., `4,850,200 EGP`).
- **Delta Indicator:** Compact pill badge displaying directional trend (`+12.4%`). Green tinted `rgba(45, 212, 167, 0.15)` with text `#2DD4A7` for positive, or coral tinted `rgba(240, 84, 107, 0.15)` with text `#F0546B` for deficit.
- **Integrated Micro Sparkline:** Ambient vector line (stroke: `#5BCBFF`, 2px width) with subtle gradient vertical fill drop (`rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.15)` down to transparent) anchoring card base.

### 3. Enterprise Dense Data Tables
- **Container:** Framed in Layer 1 glass with overflow controls.
- **Sticky Glass Header:** Sticky position, background `rgba(6, 26, 61, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border-bottom `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Typography: `label-sm` in `#8FA3C0`, text-transform uppercase.
- **Row Styling:** Height 48px (36px in compact view). Row border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`. Hover state: `rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.06)` with instant transition.
- **Numeric Cells:** Right-aligned, utilizing tabular figures with `#FFFFFF` priority and `#8FA3C0` fractional parts.
- **Status Indicators:** Pill badges (height: 22px, radius: 9999px, padding: 0 10px, typography: `label-sm`).
  - *Dispatched:* `#5BCBFF` text, `rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.12)` fill.
  - *Completed:* `#2DD4A7` text, `rgba(45, 212, 167, 0.12)` fill.
  - *Delayed:* `#F5A524` text, `rgba(245, 165, 36, 0.12)` fill.
  - *Critical Hold:* `#F0546B` text, `rgba(240, 84, 107, 0.12)` fill.

### 4. Input Fields & Select Menus
- **Base Input:** Background `rgba(4, 18, 43, 0.65)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, radius 8px, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#8FA3C0`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#5BCBFF`, box shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(91, 203, 255, 0.25)`, ambient background shift to `rgba(6, 26, 61, 0.85)`.
- **Monetary Suffix / Prefix:** Fixed inline glass badge displaying currency (e.g., `EGP`), styled in `body-sm` `#8FA3C0`.

### 5. Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
